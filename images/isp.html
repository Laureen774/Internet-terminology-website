Internet Service Provider (ISP)

Definition

An Internet Service Provider (ISP) is a company that provides individuals and organizations with access to the Internet and related services such as web hosting, email, and domain registration.

Key Features of ISPs

  • Internet Access: ISPs offer various types of internet connectivity, including dial-up, DSL, cable, fiber optic, and satellite connections.
  • Web Hosting: Many ISPs provide web hosting services, allowing customers to publish their websites on the internet.
  • Email Services: ISPs often offer email hosting solutions for their customers.
  • Domain Registration: Many ISPs provide domain registration services, allowing customers to register and manage their website domain names.
